Ice coverage datasets are linearly interpolated in time by ADCIRC, as a result, at least two datasets are required.` (410). |

| 424–441 | Method for Specifying the Coefficient of Drag / RaysIce — 통상 Garratt 항력식과 해빙(sea ice)에 관한 측정·연구를 설명한다(426–430). 기본값이 아닌 RaysIce 식과 적용 문자열을 제시한다(432–434). 최대값·대칭성과 선형 적합 연구를 설명한다(436–438). 얼음 항력계수가 Garratt 값보다 크면 대체하는 조건을 적는다(440). 원문: `The ADCIRC model ordinarily uses the wind drag coefficient formulation of Garratt (1977) in the calculation of surface wind stresses. It is a widely-used formulation and it has been found to work well for storm surge applications.` (426); `An additional physical process that has been examined in ice-covered regions such as coastal Alaska and the Great Lakes is the influence of sea ice as aerodynamic roughness elements. Macklin (1983) and Pease et. al. (1983) found that measurements of wind drag coefficients over first year sea ice typically yielded values that were significantly larger and varied less with wind speed than that predicted for open water.` (428); `More recent work (Birnbaum and Lupkes (2002) and Garbrecht et. al. (2002)) has formalized the effect of form drag on the specification of wind drag coefficients within marginal ice zones. From their work, Chapman et. al. (2005) utilized an empirical fit to the range of field data for the air-ice-water wind drag coefficient, CDF, and suggested:` (430); `CDF = [0.125 + 0.5 IC (1.0 – IC)] 10^-2 (RaysIce formulation, this is not the default)` (432); `in which IC is the ice concentration varying from 0.0 to 1.0 for open water and complete ice cover conditions, respectively. To specify this quadratic formulation for the wind drag, set the DragLawString parameter in the fort.15 file to “RaysIce”.` (434); `Inspection of the air-ice-water wind drag coefficient formula shows that a maximum value of 0.0025 occurs with 50% ice coverage. This value is very close to the Macklin (1983) measurement of 0.0028 for first year ice. Furthermore, it is seen that the value of the drag coefficient is symmetrical about 50% ice coverage suggesting that drag coefficient needed to represent 75% ice coverage is close to that of 25% ice coverage.` (436); `An alternative linear fit dependence on ice concentration has been applied by Danard et. al. (1989). These notions regarding variation of wind drag coefficient with ice cover have been supported by a number of Chukchi and Beaufort Sea storm surge simulations (Henry and Heaps, 1976; Kowalik, 1984; and Schafer, 1966) in which, wind drag coefficients greater than or equal to 0.0025 where utilized.` (438); `The method adopted for inclusion in ADCIRC considers the increased wind drag due to the presence of ice as developed by Chapman et. al. (2005). The method requires reading ice field concentration files into ADCIRC and calculating the wind drag coefficient values (variable over the model domain) using the form specified above. If ice cover is present, and the increased drag coefficient exceeds the value calculated using the standard Garratt (1997) formulation, the standard Garratt drag coefficient is replaced with the increased value associated with ice cover.` (440). |

## 판독 중 확인된 사실 (판단 아님, 후속 검토 대상)

- 410: fort.15 설정 문장은 `the ten thousands place must be set to 12`라고 적는다. 단일 자릿수 자리의 설정값을 `12`로 표현한다. 이 파일에는 언급된 NWS 값 표와 Supplemental Meteorology/Wave/Ice Parameters 표가 없다.
- 434·446: 434행은 `IC`의 범위를 `0.0 to 1.0`으로 정의한다. 446행은 같은 기호를 `IC = 50`과 `IC = 100`으로 적는다. 두 표기 사이 변환은 이 파일에 설명되어 있지 않다.
- 444·446: 444행 식에 `IC = 0`을 대입하면 `0.075 × 10^-2 = 0.00075`이다. 446행은 이때의 값으로 `(.000075)`를 적는다.
- 426·440·462: Garratt 항력식의 연도는 426행과 참고문헌 462행에서 `1977`이다. 440행은 `Garratt (1997)`로 적는다.
