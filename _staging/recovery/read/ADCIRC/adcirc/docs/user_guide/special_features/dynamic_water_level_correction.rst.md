| 1–33 | Dynamic Water Level Correction / Version — 메타데이터·앵커·제목을 포함한다(1–8). 운동량의 대기압 항과 수학적 형태가 같은 강제력(forcing)으로 공간·시간 고정 또는 가변 수위를 보정한다고 설명한다(10–18). 빠른 적용이나 개방 경계(open boundary) 연결이 약하면 입력과 달라질 수 있다는 가능성을 유지한다. 문헌 안내와 v53 초기판 사용 강한 비권고를 옮긴다(20–32). 원문: ` **Dynamic Water Level Correction** is a process by which modeled water levels ` (10); ` are dynamically adjusted by use of a forcing term. The correction can be applied ` (11); ` as constant or varying in space and/or time. The correction is applied as a ` (12); ` forcing term in the momentum equations whose mathematical form is equivalent to ` (13); ` that of an atmospheric pressure term. This means that, for gradually-varying ` (14); ` corrections, corrected water levels should closely follow the input correction, ` (15); ` though these may deviate if a correction is applied very quickly or to an area ` (16); ` that has a very weak connection to an open boundary through which water can ` (17); `` flow. Further discussion is below in the :ref:`FAQ <dwlc_frequently_asked_questions>`. `` (18); ` Early versions of this feature were implemented in v53, however important bug ` (30); ` fixes and a renaming of related variables [Note1]_ were done later, so users are ` (31); ` strongly encouraged to not use these earlier versions. ` (32). |

This should be ` (225); `    physically realistic when the water *should have* come from offshore, but if the ` (226); `    elevated water level is due to rainfall, then the model is directing flow in the ` (227); `    wrong direction. Further details are in Asher et al. 2019 [3]_. ` (228); ` ..
