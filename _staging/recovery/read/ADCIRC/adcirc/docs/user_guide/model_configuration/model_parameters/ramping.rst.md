| 26–51 | Ramping Method / 제어 항목 — NRAMP가 활성화·항목 수를 정하며 절단된 쌍곡 탄젠트(truncated hyperbolic tangent)를 일(day) 단위로 적용한다는 원문을 옮긴다(31–38). DRAMP 계열의 강제력별 이름과 FluxSettlingTime·DUnRampMete의 일 단위를 행마다 옮긴다(40–50). 원문: `` `NRAMP <NRAMP>`__ controls whether ramping is enabled, as well as how many `` (31); ` ramping terms there are. This is to permit users to apply different ramping ` (32); ` times for different forcing terms. All ramps in ADCIRC are applied as ` (33); `` (truncated) `hyperbolic `` (34); `` tangent <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_function>`__ functions over a `` (35); ` specified number of days. The various DRAMP\* variables control the number of ` (36); ` **days** over which the ramping is applied for individual forcing terms, they ` (37); ` are: ` (38); `` -  :ref:`DRAMP <DRAMP>` - any forcing terms not covered by the other DRAMP\* terms `` (40); `` -  :ref:`DRAMPExtFlux <DRAMPExtFlux>` - external flux boundary condition (BC) `` (41); `` -  :ref:`DRAMPIntFlux <DRAMPIntFlux>` - internal flux BC `` (42); `` -  :ref:`DRAMPElev <DRAMPElev>` - elevation specified BC `` (43); `` -  :ref:`DRAMPTip <DRAMPTip>` - tidal potential forcing `` (44); `` -  :ref:`DRAMPMete <DRAMPMete>` - meteorological forcing `` (45); `` -  :ref:`DRAMPWRad <DRAMPWRad>` - wave radiation stress forcing `` (46); `` Two other variables, :ref:`FluxSettlingTime <FluxSettlingTime>` and `` (48); `` :ref:`DUnRampMete <DUnRampMete>` (also in days), affect the timing of the ramping `` (49); ` terms. ` (50). |
| 52–68 | Ramping Method / 시각 조건 — NRAMP=1, 1<NRAMP<8, NRAMP=8의 콜드 시작(cold start)·FluxSettlingTime·DUnRampMete 기준을 각각 옮긴다(52–56). IBTYPE=52 복사 플럭스 경계(radiation flux boundary)의 FluxSettlingTime 의무와 기상 강제력의 핫 시작(hot start) 사용 이유를 옮긴다(58–67). 원문: ` -  If NRAMP=1, then DRAMP is relative to coldstart time. ` (52); ` -  If 1<NRAMP<8, then all DRAMP\* terms are relative to coldstart time plus ` (53); `    FluxSettlingTime ` (54); ` -  If NRAMP=8, then all DRAMP\* terms are as above, except DRAMPMete is relative ` (55); `    to coldstart plus FluxSettlingTime plus DUnRampMete ` (56); ` FluxSettlingTime is used because, for large river systems such as the ` (58); ` Mississippi River, it can take several days to initialize them and equilibrate. ` (59); ` It is a requirement when using the radiation flux boundary condition ` (60); `` (:ref:`IBTYPE <IBTYPE>`\ =52) because instabilities can quickly form otherwise. `` (61); ` DUnRampMete is useful because often a simulation is coldstarted without ` (62); ` meteorological forcing (such as a tide-only simulation), and meteorological ` (63); ` ramping is needed later when this forcing is added to a hotstarted simulation. ` (64); ` There are also namelist controls for some ramping terms, such as water level ` (66); ` offset forcing. ` (67). |

NRAMP=1·DRAMP=1 실패, 30일 선행 실행의 설정·기간 합, 후속 5일 핫 시작의 RNDAY·DUnRAMPMete·DRAMPMete, DRAMPWRad=0 이유를 원문대로 옮긴다(87–106). 이 예시는 이번 판독에서 실행하지 않았다. 원문: ` end of the river. Feeling ambitious, Arturo sets NRAMP=1 and (because he has ` (87); ` just 5 days of met. forcing) sets DRAMP=1. His model goes unstable in the ` (88); ` rivers. Now feeling cautious, he first does a 30-day simulation to ramp in the ` (89); ` river and tide forcing, with NRAMP=3, DRAMP=15 days, DRAMPExtFlux=3 days, and ` (90); ` FluxSettlingTime=10 days. This means the coldstart simulation runs with only ` (91); ` river (external flux) forcing for the first 10 days, after which the tides are ` (92); ` ramped in over 15 days, and then the model is left at full-strength forcing for ` (93); ` another 5 days to equilibrate (10+15+5=30 days). A hotstart file is generated at ` (94); ` the end of the run. Arturo uses this to hotstart his 5-day ` (95); `` (:ref:`RNDAY <RNDAY>`\ =35) storm simulation, where DUnRAMPMete=30 ensures the `` (96); ` meteorological forcing ramping starts at the 30-day mark, and DRAMPMete=0.5 ` (97); ` ensures a quick ramping of the meteorological forcing. Everything works great. ` (98); ` He's probably been overly cautious here, and might want to play around with ` (99); ` shortening some of the ramping times, but hey, at least it works. ` (100); ` Note that in the hotstart simulation, Arturo does not need to set any of the ` (102); ` other values on the DRAMP line non-zero because 30-days has already passed, and ` (103); ` so anything less than this is ignored by ADCIRC. Furthermore, note that ` (104); ` DRAMPWRad has been left at zero since ramping in the meteorological forcing ` (105); ` implies that the waves, and so the wave radiation stresses, should grow slowly. ` (106). |

## 판독 중 확인된 사실 (판단 아님, 후속 검토 대상)

- 49·56·96: 기상 지연 변수 이름은 앞에서 DUnRampMete이고 예시에서는 DUnRAMPMete로 표기되어 있다.
- 53–54·90–93: 일반 규칙은 모든 DRAMP* 항의 기준을 coldstart time plus FluxSettlingTime으로 적는다. 예시는 FluxSettlingTime=10 days로 설정하고 첫 10일에는 river (external flux) forcing만 작용한다고 적는다. 이 두 서술의 적용 관계는 이 파일에서 더 설명하지 않는다.
