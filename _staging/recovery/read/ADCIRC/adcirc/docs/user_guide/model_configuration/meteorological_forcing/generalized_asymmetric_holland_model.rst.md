The original HM was` (12); `developed to render an ideal symmetric hurricane (a.k.a., annular hurricane). To` (13); `represent hurricanes that may exhibit asymmetric structures, the Asymmetric` (14); `Holland Model (hereinafter, AHM) was developed and implemented in ADCIRC for` (15); `more practical usages [2]_, which has the same set of equations as of the HM,` (16); `but takes azimuthally-varying radius to the maximum wind to reconstruct its` (17); `spatial pressure and wind fields. Both the HM and AHM suffer from flaws that` (18); `make rendering large but weak storms difficult. As a result, efforts were made` (19); `to develop a more generalized model to fulfill the purpose. Compared to the HM` (20); `and the AHM, the GAHM removes the assumption of cyclostrophic balance at the` (21); `radius of maximum wind during derivation of its equations, and allows for a` (22); `better representation of a wide range of hurricanes. Another important feature` (23); `of the GAHM is the introduction of a composite wind method, which when activated` (24); `enables the usage of multiple storm isotaches. It makes representing complex` (25); `hurricane structures possible.` (26). |
