The finite` (19); `volume grid is discretized on a staggered Arakawa C-grid. Detailed information` (20); `about its governing equations, numerical discretization, algorithms, usage, and` (21); `tutorials is available in the **WikiROMS** documentation portal at` (22); ``**`www.myroms.org/wiki`**.`` (23); `The dynamical kernel of **ROMS** is comprised of four separate models, including` (25); `the nonlinear (**NLM**), perturbation tangent linear (**TLM**), finite amplitude` (26); `tangent linear (**RPM**), and adjoint (**ADM**). They are located in the` (27); `**Nonlinear**, **Tangent**, **Representer**, and **Adjoint** sub-directories,` (28); `respectively. The **TLM** and **ADM** were hand-coded from the discrete **NLM**` (29); `code using the recipes of Giering and Kaminski (1998). The discrete adjoint is exact and is defined relative to` (32); `the inner product that prescribes the L2-norm.` (33). |
