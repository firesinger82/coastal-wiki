| 1–3 | Big Bad Basin Test Case — 직사각형 평탄 바닥 수조(basin)에 이중 환류(double-gyre) 바람 강제력을 주는 예제이다(3). 서안경계류(western boundary current)가 중앙 Gulf Stream으로 들어가 불안정·와류(eddies)를 만든다고 적는다(3). 단열(adiabatic) 실행으로 잠재와도(potential vorticity) 균질화(homogenization)를 연구하는 목적과 SPEM에서의 이름 유래이다(3). 원문: ` This is a rectangular, flat-bottomed basin with double-gyre wind forcing. When run, it produces a western boundary current flowing into a central "Gulf Stream" which goes unstable and generates eddies. The goal is to run adiabatically to study the homogenization of potential vorticity. It earned its name by causing difficulties for the spectral versions of SPEM and taking a long time to run. ` (3). |
