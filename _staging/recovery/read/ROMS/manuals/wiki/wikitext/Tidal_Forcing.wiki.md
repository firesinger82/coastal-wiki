| 1–20 | Description of Tidal Forcing in ROMS / Astronomical Tide Generating Forces — 조석 생성력(tide generating force)은 모델 수면과 평형 조석(equilibrium tide) 수면 차의 경사로 구현한다고 적는다(1–8). TIDE_GENERATING_FORCES는 모든 셀의 경위도가 필요한 구면 격자(spherical grid)에 한정하며 경계 조석 옵션과 함께 사용할 것을 권한다(6). 수면이 평형 조석에 평행하면 측방 힘이 0이고, 평형 조석이 +x로 증가하는 예제는 +x 유동을 만든다고 설명한다(8–10). 일주·반일주(diurnal/semi-diurnal) 성분 식과 장주기 성분 제외를 적는다(11–19). 원문: `The influence of the tide generating forces ('''TGF''') can be introduced in ROMS by consideration of the gradient of the difference between ROMS sea surface, <math>\zeta</math>, and the sea surface denoted as the equilibrium tide, <math>\zeta_{EQ}</math>, which is the shape the sea surface would assume if it were motionless and in equilibrium with the tide generating forces on a fluid planet. It is activated with the option [[Options#TIDE_GENERATING_FORCES\|TIDE_GENERATING_FORCES]]. It is restricted to spherical grids since we need '''(lon, lat)''' of every cell to compute the harmonic constituents parameters for the location of the Moon and Sun. We recommend activating this option in conjunction with boundary tidal forcing ([[Options#SSH_TIDES\|SSH_TIDES]] and [[Options#UV_TIDES\|UV_TIDES]]).
