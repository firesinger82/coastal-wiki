| 31–32 | Coupled Model Parameters / 결합 방법 — 명시적 결합(explicit coupling), 사용하지 않는 암시적 결합(implicit coupling)과 반암시적 결합(semi-implicit coupling)을 설명한다(31). 반암시적 결합에서 한 방향은 명시적이고 역방향은 암시적이라고 적는다(31). 코드·설정값·예제를 원문 그대로 인용한다(31). 원문: `* Choose explicit or semi-implicit model coupling. Explicit methods require less computational effort and are accurate for small coupling time-steps. In implicit coupling (not used), the exchange fields at the next time-step (n+1) are defined by including values at the next time-step (n+1). Implicit methods are stable and allow longer coupling time-steps but are more expensive. In semi-implicit coupling, the exchange in one direction is explicit and in the reverse direction is implicit.
