# Promotion Protocol

Do not promote fresh impressions into permanent knowledge too early.

## Promote To Failure Pattern When

- the same symptom appears more than once
- or one experiment plus one source strongly support the same diagnosis

## Promote To Heuristic When

- the rule helped in more than one context
- and you can state both where it applies and where it does not

## Promote To Playbook When

- the same triage or setup sequence is repeated
- and following the sequence saves time or reduces mistakes

## Promotion Checklist

Before promotion, confirm:
- evidence exists
- scope is stated
- exceptions are stated
- confidence is stated
- linked experiments or sources are included

## What Not To Promote

- vague intuition
- single-run optimism
- explanations that cannot be tied to evidence
